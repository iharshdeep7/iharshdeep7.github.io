About

The analyst who's been on the floor

Most analysts understand supply chains through spreadsheets. I understand them through five years of managing real dispatch operations — routing fleets, coordinating vendors, flagging shortages before they became crises, and building the reports that kept leadership moving.

I came to analytics from the ground up. My MS in Business Analytics (GPA 4.0) from St. John's University gave me the technical depth. My time at Cigno Logistics gave me the operational instinct that no classroom teaches.

Today I build dashboards, data pipelines, and supply chain models that don't just show what happened — they show what's about to happen and what to do about it.
